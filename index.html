Mighty Morphin Power Rangers

Command Center Audio Deck

THE POWER RANGERS

Jason Lee Scott

Red Power Ranger

Martial artist and original team leader. Powers granted by the Tyrannosaurus Power Coin, wielding the Power Sword.

Billy Cranston

Blue Power Ranger

The brains of the team whose inventions saved the Rangers countless times. Powered by the Triceratops Coin and Power Lance.

Zack Taylor

Black Power Ranger

Creator of Hip-Hop Kido, bringing energetic fighting style and humor. Powered by the Mastodon Coin and Power Axe.

Trini Kwan

Yellow Power Ranger

Calm, compassionate, and skilled in Kung Fu. Powered by the Sabertooth Tiger Coin and Power Daggers.

Kimberly Ann Hart

Pink Power Ranger

Talented gymnast with quick reflexes and deep loyalty. Powered by the Pterodactyl Coin and Power Bow.

Tommy Oliver (Green)

Green Power Ranger

Originally created as Rita's evil Ranger before joining the team. Powered by the Dragon Dagger and Dragon Shield.

Tommy Oliver (White)

White Power Ranger

Created by Zordon and Alpha with the Light of Goodness to become the new team leader. Wields the sentient sword Saba.

DINOZORDS & MEGAZORD

Tyrannosaurus Dinozord

Pilot: Jason (Red Ranger)

A colossal robotic dinosaur that forms the torso and head of the Megazord. Wields devastating tail strikes and ground-shaking foot stomps.

Triceratops Dinozord

Pilot: Billy (Blue Ranger)

Armed with twin laser cannons and dual-towed anchor cables, this all-terrain tank forms the left leg of the Megazord.

Mastodon Dinozord

Pilot: Zack (Black Ranger)

Equipped with freezing ice-breath cannons to immobilize enemies. Forms the back, arms, and shield of the Megazord.

Sabertooth Tiger Dinozord

Pilot: Trini (Yellow Ranger)

Agile jungle crawler armed with a powerful tail cannon. Forms the right leg of the Megazord and handles high-speed maneuvers.

Pterodactyl Dinozord

Pilot: Kimberly (Pink Ranger)

Aerial combat specialist armed with laser beam projectiles. Forms the chest armor and helmet of the Megazord during combination.

The Megazord

Tank Mode & Battle Mode

When combined via "Mastodon Dinozord! Triceratops! Sabertooth Tiger! Pterodactyl! Tyrannosaurus! Dinosaurs unite!", the five Dinozords form the ultimate weapon against Rita's giant monsters, armed with the Power Sword.

Special Auxiliary Zords

Dragon Zord

Pilot:Tommy Oliver (Green Ranger)

Summoned from the ocean depths by the Dragon Dagger's tune. Features a drill tail and finger missiles, and can combine with other Dinozords into Dragonzord Battle Mode.

White Tigerzord

Pilot: Tommy Oliver (White Ranger)

Controlled by the sentient sword Saba. Converts from a lethal white tiger beast mode into a standalone Warrior Mode capable of firing fireball blasts.

ALLIES & CIVILIANS

Zordon of Eltar

Mentor & Wise Sage

An ancient intergalactic wizard trapped in a time warp. Operating from the Command Center, he selected five teenagers with attitude to defend Earth.

Alpha 5

Command Center Assistant

Zordon's loyal multi-functional robotic assistant. Known for his catchphrase "Aye-yi-yi-yi-yi!" while maintaining Command Center systems and teleportation controls.

Farkas "Bulk" Bulkmeier & Eugene "Skull" Skullovitch

Angel Grove High Bullies & Sleuths

Angel Grove High's infamous tough guy, Bulk and his sidekick Skull. Despite constant mishaps and comic relief, Bulk ultimately displays a heart of gold.

VILLAINS OF ANGEL GROVE

Rita Repulsa

Evil Intergalactic Sorceress

Imprisoned in a space dumpster for 10,000 years, she escaped to conquer Earth with her horde of monsters and the evil Green Ranger.

Lord Zedd

Emperor of Evil

The true mastermind who took over command from Rita. Armed with his Z Staff and terrifying bio-mechanical monsters.

Goldar

Winged Golden Warrior

Rita and Zedd's primary field commander. A fierce, winged warrior clad in golden armor who commands giant monster attacks.
